# Behind Every Cancer Awareness Campaign: How ACCE Volunteers Turn Information Into Impact

*Written by Elaine Ho*

Every blog you read. Every social media post you share. Every newsletter that lands in your inbox. Every educational video you watch.

Behind each one is a team of volunteers who believe that accurate information has the power to educate communities, encourage informed decisions, and support more equitable cancer care.

At the Alliance for CancerCare Equity (ACCE), awareness is more than sharing facts. It is about helping people recognize the importance of early detection, reducing stigma, and connecting communities with reliable information and support. While many people know ACCE for supporting cancer patients in Ghana and Canada, much of this work begins behind the scenes, where volunteers from different professions and backgrounds contribute their time and talents toward one shared goal:

> "Ensuring that every cancer patient receives equitable, high-quality care, regardless of their financial circumstances."

## More Than Volunteers: A Community United by One Mission

ACCE is a volunteer-led nonprofit organization made possible by people who generously contribute their time, expertise, and creativity.

Our volunteers come from across Canada, Ghana and other parts of the world, bringing together diverse perspectives and skills in healthcare, research, design, writing, communications, and technical support. While their backgrounds and roles may be different, they share one common goal: supporting ACCE's mission to advance cancer care equity.

Since 2025, about 25 volunteers have helped create educational resources, develop awareness campaigns, strengthen ACCE's digital presence, and supported the organization's online platforms.

Each volunteer brings unique skills to the mission. Healthcare professionals and researchers help ensure the information we share is accurate and trustworthy. Writers transform complex medical information into meaningful and accessible stories. Designers create compelling visuals that help important messages reach wider audiences, while video editing volunteers use creative storytelling and engaging formats to make cancer education more accessible. Technical volunteers help maintain ACCE's website, digital systems, and internal platforms, supporting effective communication and collaboration across the organization.

## From an Idea to a Campaign: The Work Behind the Scenes

A cancer awareness campaign does not begin with a finished graphic or a social media post.

It begins with an idea: how can we help more people understand cancer and access reliable information?

Each month, Dr. Ernest Osei, President and CEO of ACCE, identifies cancer awareness topics that highlight important issues and opportunities for education. From there, volunteers work together to transform each topic into meaningful resources for the community.

The first step is research.

Before creating any content, volunteers review trusted medical sources, research publications, and evidence-based information. Statistics are carefully checked, medical terms are reviewed, and information is evaluated to ensure that what we share is accurate and reliable.

Once the research is complete, the information begins to take different forms.

The **Content Design Team** creates Blogs, Newsletters, and social media captions that explain cancer topics in ways that are clear, approachable, and easy to understand. Their role is not simply to summarize medical information, but to help people connect with it.

The **Graphic Design Team** develops visuals that support these messages. Through thoughtful design and effective social media strategies, they help make important information easier to notice, understand, remember, and share with wider audiences.

The **Video Production Team** creates educational videos and patient stories, transforming information and personal experiences into engaging visual stories that help raise awareness and connect with audiences.

The **Technical Volunteer** helps maintain ACCE's digital platforms and systems, improving the user experience and ensuring that resources remain accessible to the public.

Although volunteers may work on different parts of a campaign, every project involves collaboration. Ideas are discussed, drafts are reviewed, feedback is shared, and improvements are made before content reaches the public.

## Why Awareness Matters

Cancer is more than a medical diagnosis. It affects individuals, families, and communities. Yet many people still face challenges in recognizing warning signs, accessing reliable information, or understanding available resources.

That is why awareness is such an important part of ACCE's work.

Cancer awareness is an important part of creating equitable care. Reliable information helps people recognize warning signs, understand their health options, seek support, and make informed decisions.

Beyond education, awareness also helps challenge misconceptions and reduce the stigma surrounding cancer. By sharing accurate and compassionate information, ACCE helps create more informed communities where people feel empowered to have conversations, seek care, and support those affected by cancer.

## Looking Ahead: Growing the ACCE Volunteer Movement

As ACCE continues to grow, so does our volunteer community.

In 2026, we will continue strengthening our volunteer base, expanding awareness initiatives, enhancing our digital presence, and creating more educational resources that reach communities across Ghana, Canada, and beyond.

Whether contributing through research, writing, graphic design, video production, technical support, fundraising, patient support, administration, or community outreach, every volunteer helps move ACCE's mission forward. Behind every campaign is a team of people who generously share their time, skills, and creativity because they believe everyone deserves access to reliable cancer information and equitable cancer care.
